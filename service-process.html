COUNTRY UPDATES

Important things to check before you apply.

Canada

Confirm programme and DLI status

Check that your course and institution meet current study-permit requirements before you apply.

Australia

Prepare your CoE and finances

Keep your offer, Confirmation of Enrolment and financial evidence ready for your student-visa application.

United Kingdom

Plan around course deadlines

Universities set different application deadlines, deposits and CAS timelines—start your document preparation early.

Germany & USA

Check requirements directly

Admission, language, financial and visa requirements differ by institution and programme, so always verify the latest official guidance.
