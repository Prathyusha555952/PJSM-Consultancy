Privacy Policy.

How PJSM Education collects, uses and protects your information.

Last Updated: October 2026

Introduction

PJSM Education ("PJSM", "we", "our", or "us") is an overseas education consultancy that provides guidance and support to students planning to study abroad.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website, contacting us, or requesting counselling services.

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry or request counselling, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Preferred study destination
  • Information included in your enquiry or message

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your counselling enquiry
  • Understand your study-abroad requirements
  • Provide information about courses and destinations
  • Assist with university and application-related guidance
  • Provide visa and documentation guidance
  • Contact you regarding your enquiry

Information Protection

PJSM takes reasonable measures to protect the information submitted through our website from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or misuse.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with relevant educational institutions, service providers, or other parties when necessary to provide a requested consultancy service and where appropriate.

Communication

By submitting an enquiry, you understand that PJSM may contact you using the contact information you provide in order to respond to your request or provide relevant consultancy information.

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites. PJSM is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Changes to This Policy

PJSM may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact PJSM Education through the contact details provided on our website.
