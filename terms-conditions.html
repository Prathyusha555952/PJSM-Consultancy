About Our Services

PJSM Education provides overseas education consultancy and guidance services to students considering international education opportunities.

Our services may include career counselling, course guidance, university selection, application assistance, documentation guidance and visa-related guidance.

Consultancy Guidance

Information and guidance provided by PJSM are intended to help students make informed decisions. Final admission, scholarship, visa and immigration decisions are made by the relevant universities, institutions, governments or immigration authorities.

University Admission

PJSM may assist students with applications and documentation, but admission decisions remain solely with the relevant educational institution.

Meeting minimum eligibility requirements does not guarantee admission.
