Refund Policy.

We want you to feel confident when choosing PJSM Education. Please read our refund terms carefully before making a payment for consultancy services.

15-day refund window

Eligible refund requests must be submitted within 15 calendar days of the payment date.

Terms and exclusions apply

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Our refund commitment

PJSM Education aims to explain service fees and payment terms clearly. If you have paid a consultancy service fee and wish to request a refund, you must contact us within 15 calendar days from the date of payment. Each request will be reviewed against the service already delivered and the terms agreed at the time of payment.

Please note: Submitting a request within 15 days does not automatically guarantee a full refund. The amount, if any, depends on the work already completed, the service agreement and any third-party charges.

2. How to request a refund

  • Contact PJSM Education using the email address or phone number listed on this website.
  • Include your full name, registered phone number, payment date, amount paid and proof of payment.
  • Briefly explain the reason for your request.
  • We may contact you if additional details are required to review the request.

3. Review and processing

We will acknowledge and review your request as soon as reasonably possible. If a refund is approved, we will communicate the approved amount and expected processing timeline. Bank, card, UPI or payment-provider processing times may vary and are outside our direct control.

4. Services already provided

Fees for consultancy, counselling, application support, documentation review or other work already completed may be deducted from any eligible refund, where applicable and consistent with the service agreement and applicable law.

5. Third-party fees

Payments made to universities, examination providers, government departments, visa authorities, courier services or other third parties are generally governed by those providers' own refund rules. PJSM cannot promise a refund of charges that were paid to or retained by a third party.

6. Non-refundable or special payments

Any non-refundable fee or special cancellation condition must be clearly communicated before payment and will be handled in accordance with the agreed terms and applicable law. Nothing in this policy is intended to remove any rights you may have under applicable consumer-protection laws.

7. Changes to this policy

PJSM Education may update this policy when required. The version published on this page will show the latest update date. Please keep your payment receipt and any written service agreement for reference.
